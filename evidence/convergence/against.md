# Against: the controversies and the failures

Evidence against the readings in [review](review.md).

## Against stopping at acceptable

- Schwartz 2002 measures a trait, not a project. That maximisers are less happy does not show that their results are worse (secondary).
- Satisficing is a descriptive model of people. It says what they do, and does not say that a project should do it.

## Against the optimum as the target

- Candela 2016: the balance every recommender optimises is not shown to be what developers pursue (secondary). A cost nobody checked against people yields trees nobody wants.
- The maintainer's own run found the same: the optimum was product-first and the owner's tree was stage-first (see [baseline](baseline.md)).

## Against reading the theory as a rule

- Stopping rules need a known distribution; a project has none (secondary).
- Anytime profiles are measured for algorithms. A project's quality is not a number a program returns.
- The definition of done is a convention. Schwaber 2020 gives no evidence that a team that uses one ships better; Silva 2017 is a review of its use, and its findings were not read here.

## Against a single front

- Arrow 1950 shows that choosing a point on the front is itself a social choice, so a program cannot settle it (secondary).
