# 0002: Keep the name after the frame step

**Decision:** The repository keeps its provisional name, because the field has no one word for both of its questions and no
better single name was found.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-02. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/4f99372093729dacf982e9174e94f6b8793c513d/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- Decision 0001 left the name open until the review's frame step said what the field calls the problem.
- The expressiveness frame found expressive power, succinctness and treewidth. The convergence frame found satisficing,
  stopping and anytime algorithms. They belong to different fields.
- The repository says complete and converges. No source in the first pass used either word for a model of topics.

## Decision

- Keep the name. Say on the front page and in each frame that the field's words differ.
- Use the field's words inside the two topic folders, and the repository's words only on the front page.
- Revisit the name when a second review shows one field owning both questions.

## Consequences

- A reader searching the field's words finds the frames, which list them.
- **Bad:** the repository name does not match what a search engine indexes. The frames carry the cost.
